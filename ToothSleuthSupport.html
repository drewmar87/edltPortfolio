Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock premium features?

Tap the "Practice Mode" button on the main menu, then select "UPGRADE" to unlock Palmer Notation, FDI System, and Tooth Names practice modes. Premium features are a one-time purchase with no subscriptions.

What's the difference between game modes?

Timed Mode: 90 seconds to identify as many teeth as possible. Perfect for quick practice sessions.

Survival Mode: Keep playing until time runs out. Each correct answer adds time, wrong answers subtract time.

Practice Mode: Learn at your own pace with no time pressure. Choose specific notation systems to study.

Can I play offline?

Yes! Tooth Sleuth works completely offline once downloaded. Your scores and progress are saved locally on your device.

How do I view leaderboards?

Make sure you're signed into Game Center in your device settings. Then tap "Leaderboard" from the main menu to see global rankings.

About Tooth Sleuth

Tooth Sleuth is an educational game designed to help dental and hygiene students master tooth identification. Created by a dental educator with real classroom experience, it turns learning into an engaging, competitive experience.

Features:

  • Universal Numbering System (1-32)
  • Adult and Primary Dentition
  • Multiple Game Modes
  • Global Leaderboards
  • Premium Practice Modes (Palmer, FDI, Tooth Names)
  • Clean, Professional Interface
