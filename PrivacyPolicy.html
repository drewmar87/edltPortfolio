Privacy Policy for Tooth Sleuth

Last updated: July 17, 2025

Simple Summary: Tooth Sleuth is a dental education game that respects your privacy. We don't collect personal information, emails, or track you. Game scores are handled by Apple's Game Center (if you choose to use it).

What Information We Collect

The short answer: Very little!

  • Game Center Data: If you choose to use Game Center, Apple handles your scores and achievements. We don't see your personal information.
  • Purchase Data: If you buy premium features, Apple handles the transaction. We only know a purchase was made, not your payment details.
  • No Personal Data: We don't collect emails, names, locations, or other personal information.

How We Use Information

Since we collect very little data, there's not much to use:

  • Game scores are stored locally on your device or in Game Center (your choice)
  • Premium purchases unlock additional features in the app
  • We don't send you emails, notifications, or marketing materials

Third-Party Services

Tooth Sleuth uses these Apple services:

  • Game Center: Optional leaderboards and achievements (managed by Apple)
  • In-App Purchases: Premium features (transactions handled by Apple)

These services have their own privacy policies managed by Apple. We don't control or have access to your data through these services.

Data Security

Since we don't collect personal data, there's very little to secure! Your game progress is stored on your device. Any data sent through Game Center or purchases is encrypted and handled by Apple's secure systems.

Children's Privacy

Tooth Sleuth is an educational app suitable for students of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we don't collect personal information from anyone, this isn't a concern.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we'll post the new version here and update the "last updated" date above. Since our app is simple and we don't collect much data, changes should be rare.

Educational Purpose

Tooth Sleuth was created by a dental educator to help students learn tooth identification. Our focus is on education, not data collection. Supporting the app helps support dental education.
